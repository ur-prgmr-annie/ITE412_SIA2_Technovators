Project Summary

ANIMIS is a web and mobile-based platform designed to support the operations of the Municipal Agriculture Office of Naujan, Oriental Mindoro.
It integrates animal profiling, health service monitoring, breeding records, disease surveillance, vaccine and veterinary supply 
inventory management, GIS mapping, and IoT-based cold chain monitoring. The system aims to improve data management, reduce reporting 
turnaround time, decrease record retrieval time, and support animal health services through centralized digital records and integrated 
monitoring features.
